# The Importance of a Will in Colombia: Protecting Your Assets

A will is the document by which a person disposes of their assets for after their death. Although many people postpone it, making a will prevents family conflicts and ensures that your assets are distributed according to your wishes.

## What is a will?

It is a legal act, personal in nature and generally revocable, in which a person (the testator) establishes how their assets will be distributed. It can modify the rules of intestate succession within the limits set by law.

## Types of wills in Colombia

### Open will

It is granted before a notary, who reads it and incorporates it into a public deed. Witnesses and the notary participate in its execution.

### Closed will

The testator presents the sealed document before the notary, without the notary knowing its contents.

### Solemn will

It is granted in a special ceremony before a notary and witnesses, ensuring greater formalities.

## Who can make a will?

- People over 18 years of age
- People with full mental capacity at the time of granting it

## Why is it important?

- It allows you to express your wishes about the destination of your assets.
- It avoids conflicts and disputes among heirs.
- It speeds up succession procedures.
- It protects the people you choose to benefit.

## Considerations

A will can be modified or revoked at any time while legal capacity is maintained. It is advisable to seek advice from a lawyer to draft it correctly and comply with all legal formalities.
