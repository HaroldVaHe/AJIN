# Inheritance and Succession in Colombia: Process and What to Know

When a person passes away, their assets, rights, and obligations pass to their heirs or legatees through succession. This is an important process to understand in order to protect family assets.

## What is succession?

Succession is the process by which the assets of a deceased person are transferred to those entitled to receive them. It can be:

- **Testate**: when the deceased left a will.
- **Intestate**: when there is no will and the law determines who inherits.

## Who are the heirs?

When there is no will, Colombian law establishes an order of preference:

- Spouse or permanent partner
- Children (and their descendants)
- Parents and ascendants
- Siblings and other relatives within the legal degree

## Types of succession

### Notarial succession

It is processed before a notary and is possible when all interested parties agree and are able to appear. It is a more agile alternative than a judicial process.

### Judicial succession

When there is no agreement among the heirs, or when there are minors or people who cannot appear on their own, the process must be carried out before a judge.

## General steps of the process

- Obtain the death certificate and the registry of assets
- Identify the heirs and the estate to be distributed
- Prepare the distribution of assets
- Notarize or register the adjudication
- Complete the transfer procedures before the notary and the registry of public instruments
