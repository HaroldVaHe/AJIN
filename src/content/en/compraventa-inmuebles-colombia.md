# Real Estate Purchase and Sale in Colombia: Steps and Recommendations

Buying or selling a property is one of the most important financial decisions. Understanding the process and the key documents allows you to do it safely and avoid future problems.

## Essential property documents

- **Certificate of title and freedom**: proves who the owner is and that the property is free of liens or seizures.
- **Property tax clearance**: verifies that there are no outstanding property tax debts.
- **Utility bill clearance**: confirms the status of the property's utility accounts.
- **Cadastral and property registration**: officially identifies the property.

## Steps of the purchase and sale

### 1. Verify the property

Before signing, it is advisable to review the title certificate to confirm that the seller is the owner and that there are no encumbrances.

### 2. Pre-sale agreement

This is the contract by which the parties commit to formalize the sale on a certain date. It may include payment of earnest money or a deposit.

### 3. Public deed

The transfer of ownership is formalized through a public deed granted before a notary.

### 4. Registration

The deed must be registered at the Registry of Public Instruments so that the property is held in the name of the new owner.

### 5. Delivery and clearances

Finally, the physical delivery of the property is made and the corresponding clearances are provided.

## Recommendations

- Always verify the certificate of title and freedom.
- Have a lawyer review the pre-sale agreement before signing it.
- Confirm that there are no debts associated with the property.
- Always formalize the sale through a public deed and its registration.
